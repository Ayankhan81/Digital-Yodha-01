Why Digital Safety Is
A National Need

India's rapid digital growth has transformed education, governance, healthcare, and commerce. Digital safety is a national need because it protects citizens from severe risks like financial fraud, data theft, identity theft, and cyberbullying. However, this progress has a shadow

The rise in Cyber risks — fraud, misinformation, data misuse, and exploitation — threatening the safety of millions who are coming online for the first time.

Current Reality

Students

Vulnerable to cyberbullying, gaming addiction, and online scams. Excessive screen time and lack of digital awareness can affect mental health.

Women

Frequently targeted by online harassment, cyberstalking, and identity impersonation. Privacy breaches and misuse of personal data can lead to emotional distress and safety concerns.

Seniors

High risk of financial fraud, phishing attacks, and scam calls due to limited familiarity with modern technology. Often struggle to fake links and secure their digital information.

Rural Communities

Limited awareness of digital rights, online safety, and secure internet practices. Lack of access to proper education and resources increases vulnerability to misinformation and cyber fraud.
